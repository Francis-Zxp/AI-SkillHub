AI SkillHub - sky island assets

These files are built from third-party asset packs by
scripts/sky-assets/build.mjs (see scripts/sky-assets/manifest.json).
Models were merged into libraries, unused maps removed and vertex data
optimised; at runtime they are toon shaded and buildings are recoloured.

buildings.glb, props.glb
  Medieval Village MegaKit, Fantasy Props MegaKit
  by Quaternius - CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-village-megakit
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-props-megakit
  Buildings are composed from the village kit's wall, roof, door and window
  modules (scripts/sky-assets/buildings.mjs).

villager.glb
  Animated woman from the Ultimate Modular Women pack
  by Quaternius - CC0 1.0
  https://poly.pizza/m/nIItLV9nxS

animals/*.glb (Sheep, Pig, Cow, Llama, Pug, Horse)
  Farm Animals Pack by Quaternius - CC0 1.0
  https://quaternius.com/packs/farmanimals.html
  Converted from FBX to glTF with three.js (scripts/sky-assets/convert-animals.cjs).

gull.glb
  "Flying gull" by Poly by Google - CC-BY 3.0
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  via Poly Pizza: https://poly.pizza/m/eMNhHDZakYp
  Re-exported with optimised geometry; drawn flat white and the wing beat
  animated in a shader by AI SkillHub.

Original to AI SkillHub: island terrain and rock, all trees, bushes, rocks,
grass, flowers, mushrooms and clouds (built in code), ground, water,
waterfall, cloud and sky shaders, island layout, themes and animation code.
